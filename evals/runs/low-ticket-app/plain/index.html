"Someone always ends up buying the toilet paper and nobody pays them back."

If you share a rental house, you know it. One person grabs the essentials, the receipt vanishes, and awkward texts start. Splitting every item the moment you buy it is a chore, so nobody does it.

How it works

  1. Buy something shared. Snap or type the amount and who it was for.
  2. Log it in a few taps. Everyone in the house sees it.
  3. Settle once a month. Halves nets everything out and shows who pays whom. One payment each, not twenty.
