The office manager's problem, in their words.

That check is real work: for every payment, find the procedure, look up what the contract says it should pay, compare. It is the kind of job that never makes it to the top of the list, so it does not get done.

Tidemark does the comparing. You get a short list of claims that came in under contract, with the difference on each.

How it works

Three steps. One list at the end.

01

You send the files

Your insurance remittance files, plus the contracted fee schedule for each payer you want checked.

02

Tidemark compares

Every paid line is matched to the contracted fee for that procedure and payer.

03

You get the shortfalls

Only claims paid below contract, each with what was paid, what the contract says, and the gap.
