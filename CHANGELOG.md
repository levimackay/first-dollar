# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- The skill: seven stages from a one-line idea to a landing page whose one button asks for money (a pre-order, a deposit, a paid pilot or a signed letter of intent), with two stops: the reference pick and the rough cut.
- A 46-rule lint for the patterns people read as AI-made, from default fonts and stock palettes to self-describing captions, plus the commitment check on the ask.
- A browser check: overflow from 320 to 1920 pixels, console errors, hidden or masked text, contrast, the ask above the fold, drift from the reference's colors, photo placeholders in the hero, and reduced motion.
- Twelve motion recipes, each one file that works pasted alone.
- An eval of six fictional ideas built three ways, with every page and score in `evals/`.
