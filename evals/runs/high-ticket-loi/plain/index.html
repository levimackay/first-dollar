Northstand

Steel bleachers for your fair, rented, not bought.

"Our wooden bleachers failed inspection and new ones cost more than our whole budget."

Northstand rents modular steel bleachers to county fair boards and small-town rodeos. We deliver them, set them up and have them inspected. You pay a rental fee for the season instead of buying seating you cannot afford.

Reserve the 2027 season

How it works

  • Delivered. The bleachers arrive on a trailer at your fairgrounds.
  • Set up. We assemble them where you want them.
  • Inspected. Inspection is part of the service, so you are not left holding a failed-inspection notice the week before opening.
  • Modular. Sections are sized to your crowd, so you rent what you need.

Why steel, and why rent

Wood fails inspection as it ages, and replacing it outright can cost more than a fair board's whole budget. Renting turns a large one-time purchase into one line item in the season budget. Storage, repairs and replacement are our problem.
