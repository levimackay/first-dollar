You hate the trash can, and your building won't help

You cook at home. The peels, coffee grounds and wilted herbs go in the trash because your building has no compost pickup. It feels wasteful, especially when you are paying for potting soil for the tomatoes on your balcony.

Most home composters need a yard or a hauling plan. Loam is built for a small apartment kitchen.

How Loam works

  1. Fill it. Scrape scraps into the bin on your counter as you cook.
  2. Let it work. Loam handles the rest of the process inside the sealed bin.
  3. Use it. In about 30 days you have finished compost for your pots and planters.

The goal is no smell. Today that is a result from one prototype in one kitchen, and testing with more people is what the first batch is for.
