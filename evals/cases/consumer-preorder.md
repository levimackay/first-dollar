# consumer-preorder

Fictional idea, written for this eval. No real company, person or result.

- Product name: Loam
- Idea: a countertop bin that turns kitchen scraps into finished compost in about 30 days without a smell.
- Who buys: an apartment renter who grows herbs and tomatoes on a balcony.
- Problem in their words: "I hate throwing food scraps in the trash, and my building has no compost pickup."
- Ask and price: a pre-order for $89. First units ship in spring 2027.
- Real so far: one working prototype in the founder's kitchen. No customers.
