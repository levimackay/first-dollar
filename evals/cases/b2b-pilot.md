# b2b-pilot

Fictional idea, written for this eval. No real company, person or result.

- Product name: Tidemark
- Idea: software that reads a dental practice's insurance remittance files and flags claims that were paid below the contracted fee.
- Who buys: the office manager at an independent dental practice with 2 to 6 chairs.
- Problem in their words: "I know we get shorted on claims. I do not have the hours to check every line of every EOB."
- Ask and price: a 90-day paid pilot for $1,500.
- Real so far: nothing built. The founder worked the front desk at a dental office for two years.
- Reference: https://planetscale.com
