Why shortfalls go unnoticed

Each insurance payment arrives with a remittance file or EOB listing every procedure and what the plan paid. If your practice is in-network, the plan agreed to pay a set fee for each code. When a payment lands lower than that agreed fee, nothing alerts you. The only way to catch it is to compare line by line, and that is hours of work in a week that already has none to spare.

So most offices spot-check a few, or none, and hope.

What Tidemark does

  1. You send the files

    The remittance files or EOBs you already receive from your payers, plus your contracted fee schedules.

  2. We compare every line

    Each paid amount is checked against the contracted fee for that procedure code and payer.

  3. You get a short list

    Only the claims paid below contract, with the amount short, ready to take to the payer.
